Catalyst Engine

An immersive, interactive exploration of computational chemistry and molecular discovery.

Our Mission

Bridging Education and Experimentation

The Catalyst Engine (formerly The Open Chemistry Lab) project was born from a highly motivated desire to facilitate the discovery of real-world chemical facts through hands-on experimentation. By leveraging the power of the PubChem API, we provide an accessible platform for users to manipulate elements and explore the complex properties of molecular compounds.

Architecture

Built using a robust Backend-for-Frontend (BFF) pattern, ensuring high performance and seamless data integration.
