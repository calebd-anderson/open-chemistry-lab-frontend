About Catalyst Engine

Chemistry for curious minds, designed to feel alive.

Catalyst Engine brings chemistry out of the textbook and into an interactive, exploratory lab where users can test ideas, discover new relationships, and learn by doing.

Our mission The stack

Why this exists

Built to make chemistry feel tangible.

Catalyst Engine started with a simple idea: real scientific learning becomes more meaningful when it feels hands-on. By connecting users to the PubChem API, the platform lets people explore the periodic table, test element combinations, and trace how compounds behave in a visual, approachable way.

The experience is meant to be both educational and exploratory—an environment where experimentation feels rewarding, whether you are a first-time learner or someone already fascinated by the science of matter.

The project

A student-built learning experience.

Catalyst Engine is designed as a small, creative learning platform that blends scientific inquiry with polished digital interaction.
