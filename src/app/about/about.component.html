01 The purpose

My mission

Bridging education
and experimentation.

I created Catalyst Engine, formerly The Open Chemistry Lab, to make chemistry easier to explore through hands-on discovery. You can combine elements and investigate the properties of the compounds they form.

Using the PubChem PUG REST API, I bring molecular data into an interactive lab where you can ask questions, test combinations, and learn by doing.
